# Business Agreements Reference — Atlas Crest LLC

A running checklist of the agreements and legal documents we may need as the
business grows. Keep this updated as new needs come up.

> **Note:** This is an internal planning reference, not legal advice. Before
> signing or sending any of these, have an attorney review the actual document.

---

## 1. Foundational / Internal Documents

| Agreement | What it does | When we need it |
|---|---|---|
| **Operating Agreement** | Defines ownership percentages, voting rights, profit distribution, and what happens if a member leaves the LLC. | Now — every LLC should have one even with a single member. |
| **Buy-Sell Agreement** | Sets the process and valuation method if an owner wants out, dies, or becomes disabled. | Before adding a second owner or investor. |
| **Founders / Partnership Agreement** | Splits equity, roles, responsibilities, and vesting between co-founders. | Before starting any venture with a partner. |
| **IP Assignment Agreement** | Transfers any intellectual property created by founders/contractors into the company's name. | Now, and with every new hire or contractor. |

## 2. Protecting Information & Relationships

| Agreement | What it does | When we need it |
|---|---|---|
| **NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement)** | Keeps the other party from sharing or using our confidential info. Mutual or one-way. | Before pitching ideas, sharing financials, or vetting vendors/partners. |
| **Non-Compete / Non-Solicitation** | Limits employees or partners from competing with us or poaching clients/staff after leaving. Enforceability varies heavily by state — check current law. | With key hires and partners. |
| **Confidentiality clause (embedded)** | Lighter-weight alternative — bake confidentiality terms into service and contractor agreements instead of a standalone NDA. | Default in every contract we send. |

## 3. Working with Clients & Customers

| Agreement | What it does | When we need it |
|---|---|---|
| **Master Service Agreement (MSA)** | The umbrella terms (payment, liability, termination, IP ownership) for an ongoing client relationship. | For any repeat or long-term client. |
| **Statement of Work (SOW)** | Scope, deliverables, timeline, and price for a specific project under the MSA. | Every project — prevents scope creep. |
| **Service Agreement / Client Contract** | Standalone contract for one-off engagements: scope, payment terms, late fees, kill fee, revision limits. | Any paid work without an MSA in place. |
| **Proposal / Estimate Terms** | Makes clear an estimate isn't a binding price and expires after X days. | Attached to every quote. |
| **Terms of Service & Privacy Policy** | Governs use of our website/app and how we handle user data (required if we collect any personal info). | Before launching the site or any product publicly. |
| **Refund / Cancellation Policy** | Sets expectations and protects us from chargebacks and disputes. | Before taking customer payments. |

## 4. Working with People

| Agreement | What it does | When we need it |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent Contractor Agreement** | Defines the relationship as 1099 (not employment), deliverables, payment, and IP ownership of the work product. | Every freelancer or subcontractor, before work starts. |
| **Employment Agreement / Offer Letter** | Compensation, duties, at-will status, benefits, confidentiality, and IP assignment. | First W-2 hire. |
| **Employee Handbook / Policies** | Documents workplace policies; reduces liability in disputes. | As the team grows past a few people. |
| **Commission / Referral Agreement** | Terms for paying referral fees or sales commissions. | Before promising anyone a cut of a deal. |

## 5. Vendors, Money & Property

| Agreement | What it does | When we need it |
|---|---|---|
| **Vendor / Supplier Agreement** | Pricing, delivery terms, quality standards, and remedies if a supplier fails to deliver. | Any recurring supplier relationship. |
| **Equipment Lease / Rental Agreement** | Terms for leasing equipment, vehicles, or space. | Before leasing anything in the company's name. |
| **Commercial Lease** | Office/retail/warehouse space terms — watch personal guarantees, CAM charges, and renewal options. | Before signing for physical space. |
| **Promissory Note / Loan Agreement** | Documents any money loaned to or from the company (including owner loans) with repayment terms. | Any time money moves that isn't income or a capital contribution. |
| **Personal Guarantee** | Makes an owner personally liable for a business debt — sign sparingly. | Only when a lender/landlord requires it; negotiate limits. |

## 6. Growth & Deals

| Agreement | What it does | When we need it |
|---|---|---|
| **Joint Venture Agreement** | Structures a one-off collaboration with another business: contributions, profit split, exit. | Before co-launching anything with another company. |
| **Licensing Agreement** | Lets someone else use our IP (or us use theirs) for a fee — scope, territory, exclusivity, royalties. | Before licensing our brand, content, or software either direction. |
| **Letter of Intent (LOI) / MOU** | Non-binding outline of a deal's terms before drafting the real contract. | Early stage of any acquisition, partnership, or major deal. |
| **Asset / Business Purchase Agreement** | Terms for buying or selling a business or its assets. | If we acquire or sell anything substantial. |

---

## Practices to keep us out of trouble

- **Everything in writing.** No handshake deals — even with friends and family.
- **Sign as the company.** Always sign "Atlas Crest LLC, by [name], Member/Manager" — never personally — to preserve liability protection.
- **Standard templates, attorney-reviewed once.** Build a small library of our own templates (NDA, contractor agreement, service agreement) and reuse them, instead of signing the other side's paper by default.
- **Payment terms up front.** Deposits before work starts, clear late-fee terms, and defined payment milestones on larger projects.
- **Keep a contracts folder.** Every signed agreement, fully executed with dates, stored in one place with renewal/expiry dates tracked.
- **Review before renewal.** Calendar reminders 60–90 days before any auto-renewing contract renews.
