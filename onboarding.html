Subcontractor agreement

Read this in full. Typing your legal name records your signature against this exact text, and you can ask us for a copy at any time.

1. Relationship

You are an independent contractor, not an employee of Atlas Crest LLC. You control how and when you perform accepted work, you supply your own vehicle, phone, and equipment, and you are responsible for your own taxes, insurance, and any assistants you engage.

2. No guarantee of work

Being on the Atlas Crest roster is not an offer of work and does not guarantee any volume of assignments or any level of income. Atlas Crest is under no obligation to offer you work, and you are under no obligation to accept any assignment offered. Each assignment states its fee before you decide.

3. Assignment fees

You are paid the fee stated on each assignment you accept and complete to the standard described in it. Fees for additional or revisited work are agreed in writing before that work begins.

4. Confidentiality and privacy

Property information, insured information, photographs, and client materials you receive or create are confidential and belong to Atlas Crest and its clients. You may use them only to complete the assignment. You will not retain, publish, or share them, and you will delete local copies once Atlas Crest confirms upload and review.

5. Conduct on site

You will identify yourself accurately, enter only what the assignment authorizes, and photograph only what the assignment requires. You will not photograph people, documents, plates, or interiors that the assignment does not call for.

6. Accuracy

You will record only what you actually observed and measured. You will not fabricate, reuse, or alter an observation, a photograph, a measurement, or a location.

7. Screening

Assignments are contingent on a background check and identity verification acceptable to Atlas Crest and its clients, and on those remaining current.

8. Ending the arrangement

Either party may end this arrangement at any time, with or without reason. Work you have already completed and delivered is still paid. Your confidentiality obligations continue afterward.
