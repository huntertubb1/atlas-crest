The work

What you'd actually be doing.

Residential insurance inspections

Exterior surveys of insured homes for carrier underwriting: a defined photo set, a short list of observations, an app-based report. Each stop is quick, and the work pays as a route — many inspections in a day in a compact area. No insurance background needed to start; what matters is showing up and following the checklist exactly.

Commercial inspections & audit fieldwork

Commercial property and loss-control surveys, and premium audit field work for experienced auditors. More per report, more judgment involved, and a higher documentation standard. We bring network members into this side as the volume and their track record support it.

Why work with us

How it works

From first email to steady routes.

  1. Introduce yourself

    Tell us where you're based, what your availability looks like, and any relevant experience — though for residential work, none is required. A reliable vehicle and a smartphone with a decent camera are the actual prerequisites.

  2. Background check & credentialing

    The carriers and platforms we serve require a background check before a first assignment. We set it up — it's quick and we walk you through it — and get you credentialed on the systems the work runs through.

  3. First files, closely reviewed

    You start with a small batch. We go through your first reports with you in detail — what passed, what we fixed, and why — until your files come through QA untouched.

  4. Steady routes

    As your quality holds, your volume grows. Dense, efficient routes in your area, dispatched on a predictable rhythm.
