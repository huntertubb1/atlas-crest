Privacy Policy.

How Atlas Crest LLC collects, uses, and protects the information shared with us in the course of our work.

Effective February 27, 2026.

Who we are.

Atlas Crest LLC ("Atlas Crest," "we," "us," or "our") is a Florida insurance field services firm — premium audits, risk assessments and loss-control surveys, and property inspections. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect.

We collect information directly from the people and businesses we work with — primarily policyholders, insurance carriers, MGAs, and their agents. This typically includes:

  • Business contact information (name, title, company, email, phone)
  • Audit records you provide (payroll registers, tax filings, sales summaries, certificates of insurance, and similar documents)
  • Correspondence with us by email, phone, or SMS
  • Scheduling and appointment information
  • Information you submit through our website contact form, which is delivered to our inbox by a third-party form-relay service

How we use information.

We use the information you share with us solely to perform the audit and inspection work we've been engaged to do. That includes:

  • Scheduling and conducting audits and inspections
  • Communicating with policyholders, carriers, MGAs, and agents about an open file
  • Preparing and delivering results to the engaging client
  • Maintaining our internal records of work performed

How we share information.

Findings and supporting records are shared with the insurance carrier, MGA, or vendor network that engaged us, and with their authorized intermediaries in the chain of engagement for that file. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone outside that chain of engagement.

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or legitimate regulatory request.

SMS messaging.

Atlas Crest may communicate with policyholders, agents, and carrier contacts by SMS for matters related to an active audit — appointment reminders, scheduling, records follow-up, and similar.

Mobile information and consent to receive SMS are not shared with, or sold to, any third party or affiliate for marketing purposes.

  • SMS is used only for audit-related communication with people who have given us their number in that context.
  • Message frequency varies based on the activity on your audit.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier.
  • You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP.
  • Carriers (including but not limited to AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon) are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Data security.

We treat audit records as confidential. Documents and communications are stored on access-controlled systems and shared only with the audit's chain of engagement. We do not retain audit records longer than is reasonably necessary to complete and close the audit, or as otherwise required by our engagement agreements.

Children's privacy.

Our services are for businesses and insurance professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy.

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top reflects when it was last revised. Material changes will be posted on this page.

Contact us.

Questions about this policy or about how your information is handled can be sent to office@atlascrestllc.com.
