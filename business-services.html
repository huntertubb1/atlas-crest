Service 01

A certificate proves less than you think.

A certificate of insurance proves one thing: a policy existed on the day it was printed. It says nothing about today — and nothing about the day the auditor opens your file.

So we don’t collect certificates. We check every subcontractor against Florida’s Proof of Coverage and Exemption databases — the state’s own record of who is covered and who is not. Public state data. Nothing to chase, and nothing we need from you to start.

See it on your own subs — free
Six ways a valid-looking certificate fails
+Policy cancelled before its term ended
+Dropped from a PEO policy while the policy stays active — their certificate still verifies
+Governing class code is supervisory or clerical, not the field work
+Coverage gone from the state record entirely
+Officer exemption only — covers that one person, nobody else they bring
+Exemption lapsed — Florida exemptions run two years and do not auto-renew
