Coverage & Capacity

Where we work, and what we can take on.

Headquartered on the Florida Gulf Coast, with coverage available nationwide — onsite in our home territory, and through scheduled travel, remote handling, and our managed inspector network beyond it. And a straight answer about capacity whenever you ask — we would rather turn down an assignment than miss a due date on one.

Onsite

Florida Gulf Coast.

Physical audits and inspections are handled directly by us across west and southwest Florida, plus the inland counties between them.

Primary territory

  • Tampa & St. Petersburg
  • Sarasota & Bradenton
  • Port Charlotte & Punta Gorda
  • Fort Myers & Naples
  • Lakeland, Bartow & the inland counties
  • Arcadia, DeSoto & the surrounding rural corridor

Extended reach

The primary territory is where we're based, not the limit of where we work. We regularly schedule work beyond it — the Florida Panhandle, the east coast, and out of state — when the volume and timing support the trip.

If you have work outside these areas, ask. We'll take it directly, route it through our network, or tell you plainly that we're not the right fit.

Remote

Nationwide, without the travel.

A large share of this work no longer requires anyone to be in the room. For those services, geography isn't a constraint.

Virtual & phone audits

Records reviewed remotely with a live interview, handled for carriers and vendor networks in any state.

Mail & flat-rated files

Low-exposure and flat-rated audits handled at volume without onsite time.

Scheduling & insured contact

First contact, records requests, and appointment handling run from our office on every file — so field days stay field days.

Central QA & file review

Every network report crosses our desk before it goes back to the client — one standard, no matter who was onsite.

Capacity

Our inspector network.

Field work is limited by how many people can be in how many places. We're building a vetted network of independent inspectors and auditors so vendor partners can get coverage past our own driving radius.

We'll always tell you what's ours and what's the network's. Every person working under Atlas Crest is background-checked before an assignment, works to the same reporting standard, and every file is reviewed by us before it goes back to you.

For vendor partners

If you have persistent coverage holes in a territory, tell us where. Building toward known demand is far more useful than recruiting at random, and we'd rather grow into your gaps than guess at them.
