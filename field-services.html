Field Services

Assign it once. Get it back complete.

Premium audits, risk assessments and loss-control surveys, and property inspections — residential and commercial — for carriers, MGAs, and national vendor networks. We show up on schedule, document what matters, and return a file your reviewer doesn't have to send back.

Service line

Premium audit services.

A policy is priced on an estimate. The audit replaces that estimate with what actually happened — the payroll that was really paid, the sales that were really made, the exposures that really existed.

Our job is to get that right and make it verifiable: figures tied to source records, classifications applied the way the manual and the state require, and a file that documents how we got there.

Lines we handle

  • Workers' compensation — monoline and package
  • General liability — payroll, sales, and unit bases
  • Specialty and miscellaneous commercial lines
  • Final, interim, cancellation, and reopened audits

Methods

  • Physical — onsite with the insured and their records
  • Virtual — records reviewed remotely with a live interview
  • Phone and mail audits on flat-rated and low-exposure files
  • Multi-state and multi-location handling where the policy calls for it
How a file runs

From assignment to submission.

  1. Contact goes out the day we're assigned

    The insured hears from us immediately, with a clear explanation of what the audit is, exactly which records we need, and a proposed time. Fast, specific first contact is most of what keeps a file on schedule.

  2. Records get reviewed against the policy

    Payroll registers, tax returns, certificates, and ledgers are reconciled to each other and to what the policy actually rates on — not accepted at face value.

  3. Questions get asked while we're still in it

    Job duties, ownership structure, subcontractor use, and anything that drives a classification get settled with the insured directly, before the file closes rather than in a dispute afterward.

  4. The file goes back complete

    Worksheets that balance, documentation attached, notes written to the carrier's standard, and findings explained clearly enough that the next person to open it doesn't need to call us.

Service line

Risk assessments & loss control surveys.

Before a carrier insures a risk — or while the policy is in force — someone needs to go look at it. We put trained eyes on the property and the operation, document how the risk is actually being managed, and report what's really there.

Most of this work is assigned by carriers, MGAs, and vendor networks: underwriting surveys, in-force loss control visits, and follow-ups confirming that prior recommendations were actually completed. Businesses can also commission a survey directly — the same walkthrough and hazard documentation, done for an owner who wants a clear picture of their exposures before the carrier's inspector, or their renewal, shows up first.

What we document

  • Construction, occupancy, protection, and exposure
  • Fire and life-safety systems and their condition
  • Housekeeping, maintenance, and management practices
  • Hazards specific to the operation and how they're controlled
  • Status of previously issued recommendations

What you get back

  • Photo-supported findings, captioned and organized
  • Practical recommendations an insured can actually act on
  • Clear severity, so an underwriter can triage quickly
  • A report written to be read, not decoded
Service line

Commercial property inspections.

Underwriting inspections that give the file what it needs to price and manage the risk.

COPE data

Construction, occupancy, protection, and exposure captured accurately and consistently — the foundation everything else in the file rests on.

Condition & risk

Roof, systems, and general condition documented with photos, along with anything that materially changes how the risk should be viewed.

Occupancy verification

Replacement-cost detail

Measurements and construction detail captured to support a replacement-cost estimate and flag under-insurance.

Service line

Residential underwriting inspections.

High-volume exterior surveys of insured homes for carrier underwriting — a defined photo set, a consistent observation checklist, and app-based reporting, worked as dense routes rather than one-off stops.

What gets captured

  • Dwelling condition and visible maintenance issues
  • Roof condition as observable from the ground
  • Hazards — pools, trampolines, dogs, outbuildings, debris
  • Occupancy and use, as observed against the application

How volume is handled

Routes are dispatched through our managed network of vetted, background-checked independent inspectors — each working the same checklist and photo standard, with every report reviewed at our desk before it goes back to the client. One firm to assign, one standard on the file, whatever the count.
