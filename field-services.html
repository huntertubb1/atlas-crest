Service 02

Loss control that a policyholder can act on.

Onsite fire, life-safety, and hazard surveys. We document how the risk is protected and managed today, photograph what matters, and separate what is required from what is advisable.

Recommendations are written to be completed, not filed — specific, priced in effort, and tied to the exposure they reduce.

+Fire protection, detection, and alarm condition
+Housekeeping, storage, and hot-work practice
+Egress, life safety, and occupancy hazards
+Prior recommendations, verified or still open

Service 03

Commercial property inspections, captured for underwriting.

COPE data, replacement-cost detail, occupancy, and condition — measured and photographed onsite, then written up so the underwriter is not guessing at what the building actually is.

C

Construction

Frame, walls, roof covering and deck, year built, and any additions.

O

Occupancy

Actual use by tenant and area, including anything not on the application.

P

Protection

Sprinklers, alarms, hydrant distance, and responding department.

E

Exposure

Neighboring risks, separation, and site conditions around the building.
