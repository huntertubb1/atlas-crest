About

Atlas Crest LLC.

A Florida insurance field services firm. We handle premium audits and property inspections for carriers, MGAs, and vendor networks — directly on the Florida Gulf Coast, and through a managed network of vetted independent inspectors beyond it. Founded in 2026, with a growing practice.

Our story

A firm built around how the work should be done.

Insurance is a relationship business — and like any relationship, it runs on trust. Trust that people will do what they say. Trust that the person on the other side of the file knows their work. Trust that when questions come up, they'll be answered honestly.

Atlas Crest was founded to be the kind of firm that earns that trust quietly, one file at a time. We took the long view from day one. The reputation we're building now is one we plan to live up to for years.

What we learned doing it

From the field up.

We started in premium audit — going through payroll records, applying classifications, and returning files that carriers could rely on without a second pass.

Doing that work teaches you what actually makes a file good: contact that goes out the day it's assigned, figures that tie to source records, documentation to the client's standard, and findings written so the next reader doesn't need to call. None of that depends on who drove to the site. All of it depends on who sets the standard and who reviews the file.

That's the firm we're building: the standard and the review stay at our desk, and a managed network of vetted independent inspectors puts more people in more places than we could ever drive to ourselves. Each new territory starts with residential inspection routes and grows into commercial and audit work as the bench proves out.

Our values

What we hold ourselves to.

Integrity

Our work reflects what actually happened. We don't shade findings to make a file easier, and we don't paper over questions to make a deadline.

Quality

Every file is reviewed thoroughly before it leaves us. We measure ourselves on getting it right the first time.

Transparency

Clients understand what we're doing, why we're doing it, and how it affects them. No surprises, no guesswork.

Service

Questions get answered the same business day. We move work along steadily so no one is waiting on us.

Where we're headed

A modern firm, built the old-fashioned way.

We built our own back office on software we wrote for it, because a practice like this lives or dies on details that are easy to drop — an unanswered request, an unscheduled route, a deadline two weeks out. Systems handle the tracking and the reminders. The judgment stays with a person.

Our goal is simple: be the firm people trust because we do what we say, and we do it well. Every territory and service line we add will be held to that same standard — dispatched by us, worked to our checklist, and reviewed at our desk before it ships.
