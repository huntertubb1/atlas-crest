About Atlas Crest

We have sat on the other side of the desk.

Atlas Crest is a Florida insurance services firm built around premium audit work. We spend our days in payroll records, class codes, and certificates of insurance — the documents that decide what a policy is worth and what a business ends up paying.

That experience runs in one direction and then the other. For carriers, MGAs, and vendor networks, we conduct the audits and inspections that determine exposure. For businesses, we prepare the records those audits examine, and keep the subcontractor file current so an uninsured job never becomes their payroll.

Knowing what gets charged is the whole advantage. A file that looks complete and a file that survives an audit are two different files, and we can tell them apart because we have written the findings ourselves.

Four ideas shape every file we touch

Integrity

We tell the truth about what we find, whether or not it is the answer anyone wanted.

Quality

We take the time to get it right the first time, because a reopened file costs everyone more.

Transparency

We explain what we are doing in plain language, including the parts that are not good news.

Service

We respond quickly and treat every client and insured with respect.

How the firm runs

We run our own back office on software we built for it. Documents are read and filed within minutes of arriving, every open item carries a follow-up date, and nothing closes until the file is complete.

The judgment calls stay with a person. Whether a certificate genuinely covers the work, how a class applies to an operation that does three things at once — those are decided by someone who has done the audit, not by a rule in a system.

What that means in practice
+Inquiries answered promptly, by the person handling the work
+Audit files returned complete, with the basis documented
+Inspection dates committed when we accept the assignment
+Renewals chased before the lapse, not after
+Every judgment call approved by a person

An audit assignment, a stack of certificates nobody has looked at, or a territory you need covered.
