The finding we see most

An uninsured sub becomes your payroll.

You asked for a certificate. You got one. Then the policy lapsed mid-job, or it never covered the trade the work fell under. Nobody was watching the dates.

At audit, every dollar you cannot document gets reclassified to your payroll at your rate. We have sat in the auditor's chair and written that charge. Now we work the other side of it.

How certificate tracking works

Certificate tracking, step by step

Documents are read and filed within minutes of arriving. Every open item carries a follow-up date, and nothing closes until the file is complete.

Step one — collect

We take your vendor list and go after every certificate on it directly, so your team is not the one sending reminder emails.

  • +Requests sent to each sub and their agent
  • +Existing files inventoried against what you paid out
  • +Missing documents logged with an owner and a date

Step two — verify

A certificate on file is not the same as a certificate that holds up. We check what an auditor checks.

  • +Policy term covers the period the work was performed
  • +Coverage matches the trade, not just the name
  • +Exclusions and endorsements read, not skimmed

Step three — chase

Renewals are pursued before the lapse, not discovered after it. Nothing sits waiting on a desk.

  • +Expiry dates tracked with advance follow-up
  • +Cancellations caught and escalated same day
  • +Gaps flagged while you can still fix them

Step four — hand over

When the audit is scheduled you hand over one complete file instead of assembling it under a deadline.

  • +Certificates indexed to the payments they cover
  • +Payroll split by class code
  • +Officer exclusions and waivers documented
