How we work

One firm to assign. A network to deliver.

Field work is limited by how many people can be in how many places. Our answer is a managed network: Atlas Crest takes the assignment, dispatches it to a vetted, background-checked independent inspector, and holds every report to one standard — ours.

You deal with one firm. One point of contact, one reporting standard, one invoice — whether the file was worked by us or by the network. Scheduling and insured contact run from our office, and no report goes back to a client until it has been reviewed at our desk.

We'll always tell you plainly what's ours and what's the network's. On the Florida Gulf Coast, the person at the door is usually us. In the territories we're building, it's our network — working our checklist, reviewed by our QA.

Why us

We know why files get sent back.

We work these files for a living — which means we know exactly what a reviewer looks for, because it's what we look for.

A file that doesn't bounce

Most kicked-back reports fail on the same few things: figures that don't tie to source records, photos that don't support the findings, special instructions that weren't followed as written. Those are exactly the checks every file clears before it leaves us — whoever worked it.

  • Figures tied to source records, not taken at face value
  • Photos and documentation to the client's standard
  • Findings written so the next reader doesn't need to call

Nothing waits on a desk

We run our own back office on software we built for it. Documents are read and filed within minutes of arriving, every open item carries a follow-up date, and nothing closes until the file is complete. The judgment calls — how a class applies, whether a finding is worth flagging — stay with a person who knows the difference.

  • Insured contact goes out the day we're assigned
  • Every follow-up tracked — nothing gets forgotten
  • A person approves anything that takes judgment
